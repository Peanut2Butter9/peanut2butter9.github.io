Research Notes on Modular Forms and Hecke Operators

Published: October 22, 2026 · 139 pages

Download the notes from here [version 20261022] (PDF, 1.2 MB).

  • Update 2026/10/22: First version posted.

This is about the handwritten notes turned into a typeset LaTeX version when I was participating in YSEP at Institute of Science Tokyo, learning about modular forms under the supervision of Dr. Soma Purkait and Prof. Yuichiro Taguchi.

The goal was actually to understand the further study in modular forms, as I have been working on since my bachelor's thesis, to a more generalization. Dr. Soma once suggested the goal was to understand from modular forms to their L-functions (which generalize the Riemann zeta function), but as the research period was coming to an end, there was not enough time to reach the goal and stop at Hecke operators.

Most of the material follows Dr. Soma’s lecture notes from the modular forms course she taught a few years ago, together with Koblitz’s book, with some help from Diamond–Shurman and Serre.

Yes, shout-out to Orlando Ferrari’s lecture notes project, which inspired the way I made and wrote up these notes. (His website is cool, please look at it)

I don't claim my notes were perfect; please read and double-check the words; any mistakes are mine. If you spot one, feel free to reach out to me on GitHub.

Grateful to Dr. Soma Purkait for accepting me as a research student under her supervision and for all her guidance throughout this program, as well as Prof. Yuichiro Taguchi for formally taking me under his name in this program, which made this research stay possible in the first place.
